# Data governance

1. Do not commit client files, personally identifying information, credentials, or confidential case materials.
2. Record source, date, license, permitted uses, and provenance for every dataset.
3. Separate public legal materials from private client documents and stylistic examples.
4. Do not use private matter data for training without a valid legal basis and appropriate informed authorization.
5. Apply minimization, anonymization where appropriate, access controls, retention limits, and deletion procedures.
6. Review copyright, database rights, confidentiality, and applicable Egyptian data-protection requirements before ingestion.
7. Keep dataset manifests and audit records; never treat an unverified source as approved.
