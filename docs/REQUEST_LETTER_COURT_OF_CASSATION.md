# Draft request for institutional permission — Egyptian Court of Cassation

**To:** The competent authority responsible for the Court of Cassation website and its legal information services  
**Subject:** Request for written institutional authorization and technical licensing for lawful use of published judgments in an Egyptian legal AI research and assistance platform

Dear Sir/Madam,

We are developing **AMIN Legal Model**, an Egyptian-law artificial intelligence engine intended to support legal research and analysis across all branches of Egyptian law. We wish to obtain formal written guidance and, if available, an institutional license or authorized technical access arrangement for the Court of Cassation materials published through the Court's official portal.

We have reviewed the portal's published terms and understand that a personal subscription does not itself authorize automated extraction or commercial republication, and that prior written approval is required for commercial use/publication. Accordingly, we will not conduct scraping, bulk extraction, or commercial reuse of portal materials unless expressly authorized in writing by the competent authority.

We respectfully request confirmation whether the Court can grant an institutional permission, license, API, or authorized bulk-data delivery arrangement covering the following activities, to the extent the Court is legally able to authorize them:

1. Access to and receipt of specified published judgments, principles, headnotes, and metadata, including civil, commercial, economic, labor, personal-status, criminal, and other available divisions.
2. Authorized digital copying, storage, backup, preservation, OCR, text correction, and normalization within a controlled legal-research database.
3. Indexing, full-text search, chunking, embeddings, retrieval, and AI-assisted legal analysis for responses to authenticated platform users.
4. Displaying appropriately limited excerpts, references, source links, court/circuit/date details, and docket or appeal identifiers to users of a commercial legal-assistance service, with attribution and any limits required by the Court.
5. Authorized periodic updates, corrections, replacement records, and withdrawal/deletion notices.
6. Technical access method, including API, licensed bulk export, or another method designated by the Court, together with rate limits and security requirements.
7. **Separate clarification** on whether any permission may extend to model training or fine-tuning. We will not use the materials for training/fine-tuning unless this activity is expressly authorized in writing.

Please also clarify:

- Which categories and date ranges of material can be licensed, and any exclusions.
- Whether the Court controls the relevant rights or whether another entity's permission is also required.
- Permitted quotation/display limits, mandatory attribution, linking, and restrictions on user downloads or redistribution.
- Whether the license may cover a commercial SaaS/API offered to lawyers and legal professionals, and any restrictions on the number or type of users.
- Applicable fees, subscription/licensing structure, contract duration, renewal, termination, and post-termination data deletion/retention requirements.
- The official procedure, forms, documents, and authorized department/contact for submitting a formal application.

We are prepared to provide a technical description of the platform, proposed safeguards, sample attribution format, data-security controls, and a narrowly scoped pilot proposal for the Court's review.

Until written authorization is received and its scope is confirmed, we will respect the portal's existing terms and will not automate collection or incorporate restricted materials into the production corpus.
